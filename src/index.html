About Apex

Apex is a client-side web application designed to parse, analyze, and visualize karting telemetry data. Upload your session data to uncover insights.

  • Local CSV Upload
  • Dynamic Leaderboard
  • Visualizations
  • Track Map Rendering
  • GPX and GPS CSV Parsing
  • Speed Gradients
  • Ghost Sync Replay
